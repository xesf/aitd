Alone in the Dark

Import the folder of an installed game: Alone in the Dark 1, 2 or 3, Jack in the Dark, or Time Gate. The files stay in this browser and are not uploaded. Saves are kept with them.

    Keys: arrows walk, Space acts, Enter opens the inventory, Esc opens the menu, 1 to 0 and - choose an action. A gamepad works too. Time Gate's music needs a General MIDI soundfont (.sf2) in the game folder.
